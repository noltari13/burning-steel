Area templates

Nested-ring hex overlays (Area Sizes rules): Targeted = red hex; Small adds light red; Medium adds orange; Large adds yellow. Flat-top hexes, transparent background. Regenerate whenever the rules' area shapes change, then publish to tts/assets/png/<next version>/ — never overwrite a published version (TTS caches images by URL forever).
